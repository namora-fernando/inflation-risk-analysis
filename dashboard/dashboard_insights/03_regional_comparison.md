# Regional Comparison Analysis  

This page focuses on comparing inflation risk conditions at the regional level and positioning the selected region relative to other global regions in the same year. The example shown in this view based on **Sub-Saharan Africa (2023)**, but all insights dynamically update with the region and year slicers.

---

![Regional Comparison](../dashboard_pages/regional_comparison.png "Regional Comparison")

---

## Key Regional Snapshot (Sub-Saharan Africa, 2023)

- **Average Risk Score:** 59.20  
- **Average CPI Annual Change:** 14.82%  
- **Average Exchange Rate Change (YoY):** 26.43%

These values indicate that in 2023, Sub-Saharan Africa experienced a combination of elevated inflation, significant currency pressure, and high overall composite risk relative to most other regions.

---

## Regional Risk Ranking (2023)

For the selected year on slicer, the bar chart *Average of Risk Score by Region* shows that in **2023**:

- **Middle East & North Africa** ranks as the highest regional risk cluster.
- **Sub-Saharan Africa** follows closely as the **second-highest risk region globally**.
- Other regions such as South Asia, North America, and East Asia & Pacific remain in a similar mid-to-high risk band.
- **Europe & Central Asia** appears at the lower end of the regional risk spectrum.

This positioning confirms that Sub-Saharan Africa is consistently one of the most vulnerable regions in terms of inflation-related macroeconomic risk.

---

## Risk Category Composition by Region (2023)

The stacked bar chart illustrates how countries within each region are distributed across **High, Medium, and Low risk categories**:

- In **Sub-Saharan Africa**, approximately:
  - **50%** of countries fall into the **High-risk** category  
  - **28.26%** are classified as **Medium risk**  
  - Only **21.74%** remain in the **Low-risk** group  

- Other regions such as **South Asia** and **Latin America & Caribbean** also exhibit a large concentration of high-risk economies, though with slightly more balanced distributions.
- **Europe & Central Asia** shows the most stable profile, with a majority of countries in the Medium and Low risk bands.

This confirms that Sub-Saharan Africa not only ranks high in average risk score, but also has a **structurally heavier concentration of high-risk countries**.

---

## Interpretation and Analytical Context

Sub-Saharan Africa’s elevated regional risk is driven by the combination of:

- High inflation volatility across multiple economies  
- Strong exchange rate depreciation pressures  
- Structural vulnerabilities captured through the composite risk score construction  

The regional comparison highlights that inflation risk is **not evenly distributed globally** and instead clusters geographically, with Sub-Saharan Africa emerging as one of the most persistent high-risk regions.
